Frequently asked questions.

01What is onAir 5G?

A USB-C 5G device that gives your MacBook its own mobile connection. It sits flush with your Mac and shows up in the menu bar, just like the 5G icon on your phone.

02Do I need to install anything?

No. Plug it in and you're online. The menu bar app is optional and adds signal, data left, SIM choice and top-ups.

03Is data included?

Yes. onAir comes with starter data that activates the first time it connects. Amount and validity to be confirmed.

04Is there a subscription?

No. It's pay as you go: when the included data runs out, top up in two clicks from the app. Prefer your own carrier? Install your eSIM or drop in your SIM. Top-up prices to be confirmed.

05Which countries does it work in?

The included data works in 150+ countries*. You can also use your own eSIM or drop in a local SIM. Link to the full country list.

06Will it take over my Wi-Fi?

No. Wi-Fi stays first. onAir is there when Wi-Fi isn't, or when you'd rather not use it.

07Does it stay connected with the lid closed?

Yes, with Keep Awake turned on in the app. Your agents, syncs and uploads keep running.

08Which computers does it work with?

To be confirmed: supported MacBook models, macOS versions, and whether Windows and iPad are supported.
