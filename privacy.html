Who we are

The onAir 5G waitlist is operated by Ferraris Group Sagl, Corso San Gottardo 99, 6830 Chiasso, Switzerland, trading as Rolling Square ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. For anything privacy-related, write to support@rollingsqua.re.

This policy covers the onAir 5G waitlist specifically. Our general store privacy policy is available at rollingsquare.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Email address — when you join the waitlist. Used to confirm your spot and send you onAir 5G updates and launch communications. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Phone number (optional) — only if you choose to add it after joining. Used for SMS updates about onAir 5G. Legal basis: your consent. See "SMS terms" below.
  • Deposit payments — if you reserve with the $20 deposit, payment is processed entirely by Stripe. We receive confirmation of your payment and your email; we never see or store your card details. Legal basis: performance of a contract (your reservation).
  • Technical data — our hosting provider processes standard server logs (IP address, browser type) to deliver the page. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this page today; if that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Who processes data for us

  • Klaviyo, Inc. (USA) — email/SMS list management and sending.
  • Stripe, Inc. (USA/Ireland) — deposit payment processing.
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) — page hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • Google LLC (USA) — web font delivery (Google Fonts).

Transfers outside Switzerland/the EEA rely on these providers' standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards.

SMS terms

Adding your phone number is optional and is not a condition of joining the waitlist or of any purchase. By adding it you agree to receive recurring marketing text messages about onAir 5G from Rolling Square at the number provided. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.

How long we keep your data

Waitlist data is kept until the onAir 5G launch cycle ends or until you unsubscribe, whichever comes first, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Payment records are kept as long as accounting law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, the Swiss FADP and the CCPA), you can ask us for access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to support@rollingsqua.re and we'll take care of it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & local storage

This page does not set advertising or analytics cookies. We use your browser's local storage for one thing only: remembering that you joined the waitlist, so the page can show you the next step instead of the form again. It stays on your device and is never sent to us; you can clear it anytime from your browser settings.

If you open the Stripe checkout, Stripe sets its own cookies on its own domain for payment security and fraud prevention — see Stripe's privacy policy.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top changes with it; material changes will be announced to the waitlist by email.
